01 / Intelligent Assessment

From network risk to the exact region that needs attention.

The assessment layer combines existing infrastructure data, structural engineering, and targeted sensing so high-resolution inspection is focused where it matters most.

A
Network Risk PrioritizationCondition, age, material, loading, repair history, environmental exposure, hazards, and consequence.
B
Critical Zone PredictionUse drawings, load paths, structural analysis, and deterioration history to identify high-risk regions.
C
Targeted Robotic NDEDeploy methods such as ultrasound, impact echo, GPR, thermal imaging, and computer vision where needed.
D
Damage & Repair PrioritizationRank defects by severity, structural importance, progression risk, and suitability for targeted repair.

02 / Biological Restoration

Deliver the repair agent directly into damaged concrete.

Rather than treating every surface equally, confirmed internal damage becomes a precise intervention target. Vascular access creates a pathway into subsurface crack networks.

1
Target the confirmed defectNDE defines the location and extent of the damaged region.
2
Create vascular accessControlled drilling intersects subsurface crack networks while minimizing unnecessary disturbance.
3
Deliver biological healing mediaAgar hydrogel transports bacteria and nutrients through the damaged region while retaining moisture.
4
Promote mineral formationBiomineralization produces calcium carbonate within the crack network to seal damaged pathways.
03 / Validation & Feedback

Inspection does not stop after the repair.

The same sensing framework returns to the repaired region to verify change and improve future maintenance decisions, turning inspection and repair into a closed feedback loop.

1
ReinspectRepeat targeted NDE at the repaired region.
2
CompareEvaluate before-and-after condition indicators and defect response.
3
UpdateFeed validated condition information back into the asset model and future prioritization.

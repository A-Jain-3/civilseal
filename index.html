01 / Intelligent Assessment

From infrastructure networks to individual hidden defects.

CivilSeal combines existing infrastructure data, structural engineering analysis, and targeted nondestructive evaluation to determine which assets require attention and exactly where to inspect.

1A. Network Risk Prioritization

Bridge condition, material, age, structural type, traffic, environmental exposure, repair history, and hazard information are combined to identify assets requiring attention first.

1B. Critical Zone Prediction

Structural models, drawings, load paths, deterioration history, and engineering analysis identify the most critical regions of the structure.

1C. Targeted Robotic NDE

Robotic platforms deploy complementary methods such as ultrasound, impact echo, ground penetrating radar, thermal imaging, and computer vision in priority regions.

1D. Damage & Repair Prioritization

Detected defects are evaluated by severity, structural significance, progression risk, accessibility, and suitability for targeted repair.

02 / Biological Restoration

Deliver repair directly into hidden cracks.

Once damage has been identified, targeted vascular access creates a pathway between the concrete surface and internal cracks, allowing biological healing agents to reach the damaged region directly.

Targeted Vascular Access

Engineered channels create controlled access from the concrete surface into identified subsurface crack networks.

Hydrogel-Based Delivery

Agar hydrogel transports bacteria and nutrients, retains moisture, and helps distribute the healing medium throughout the crack network.

Biological Mineralization

Ureolytic bacteria promote calcium carbonate precipitation inside the damaged region.

Internal Crack Sealing

Mineral deposition occurs throughout the crack network, creating targeted repair from within the concrete.

03 / Validation & Feedback

Repair is not complete until it is verified.

CivilSeal closes the loop by reinspecting repaired regions and comparing their post-repair condition with the original damage state.

Follow-Up NDE

The repaired region is rescanned using complementary nondestructive evaluation methods.

Before / After Comparison

Inspection data are compared to evaluate changes in the damaged region following repair.

Condition Model Update

New inspection and repair-performance information is returned to the infrastructure condition model.

Continuous Feedback

Each intervention creates new information for future inspection and maintenance decisions.

Research Foundation

Built from published concrete repair research.

The biological restoration concept builds on published research demonstrating that targeted artificial vascular channels can improve the delivery of hydrogel-supported biological healing agents into damaged cementitious materials.

CivilSeal extends this concept toward a complete infrastructure system linking damage identification, targeted repair, and post-repair verification.

≈100%

Healing-agent distribution

Near-complete distribution through artificial crack networks was observed in tested laboratory conditions using drilling-assisted vascular access.

≈3.5×

Greater mineral precipitation

Drilling-assisted specimens produced substantially greater calcium carbonate precipitation than specimens without vascular access in the reported laboratory study.

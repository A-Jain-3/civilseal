01 / DETECT

Know where to look.

CivilSeal narrows a large infrastructure network down to the specific structural regions where detailed inspection and intervention matter most.

1A. Network Risk Prioritization

Condition, material, structural type, age, loading, repair history, environment, and hazards are used to prioritize infrastructure assets.

1B. Critical Zone Prediction

Structural models and load paths identify regions where deterioration may have the greatest impact.

1C. Targeted Robotic NDE

Robotic platforms investigate priority regions using complementary nondestructive evaluation methods.

1D. Damage Prioritization

Detected defects are evaluated by severity, structural significance, progression risk, and repair suitability.

02 / RESTORE

Repair from within.

Once damage is identified, targeted vascular access creates a pathway to the internal crack network and delivers biological healing agents precisely where they are needed.

Targeted Vascular Access

Engineered micro-channels connect the concrete surface to subsurface damage.

Hydrogel Delivery

Agar hydrogel carries bacteria and nutrients while retaining moisture within the crack environment.

MICCP

Ureolytic bacteria promote calcium carbonate formation inside the crack network.

Targeted Crack Sealing

Mineral precipitation develops within the damaged region, creating a biologically driven repair pathway.

03 / VERIFY

Prove the repair worked.

CivilSeal closes the loop by returning to repaired regions with nondestructive evaluation and comparing their condition with the original damage state.

Reinspect

Scan the repaired region using complementary NDE.

Compare

Compare pre-repair and post-repair condition maps.

Update

Feed verified condition data back into the infrastructure model.

Learn

Use repair outcomes to improve future prioritization and intervention decisions.

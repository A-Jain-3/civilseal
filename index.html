Restore

Repair from within.

Once NDE confirms the weak point, CivilSeal creates localized vascular access into the existing subsurface crack network and delivers a biologically active healing medium directly where it is needed.

The key innovation: targeted delivery

Instead of broadly treating large concrete surfaces, CivilSeal creates localized access to hidden cracks and delivers the healing material deep into the damage zone.

Targeted Vascular Access

Small engineered channels connect the concrete surface directly to existing subsurface crack networks.

Hydrogel Biological Delivery

Agar hydrogel carries bacteria and nutrients into the targeted internal crack network.

Biological Mineralization

Ureolytic bacteria promote microbially induced calcium carbonate precipitation inside the cracks.

Internal Crack Sealing

Calcium carbonate develops within the damaged region rather than only at the visible surface.

Robotic Delivery

A robotic platform localizes confirmed damage, creates targeted vascular access, delivers the healing agent, and supports post-repair verification.

Research Foundation

Detection science meets biological concrete repair.

CivilSeal connects nondestructive evaluation with emerging vascular biological repair. Complementary sensing technologies identify and characterize deterioration, while targeted biological intervention provides a pathway for repairing confirmed internal damage.

Nondestructive Evaluation

Different defects require different ways of seeing.

CivilSeal combines complementary NDE technologies to characterize deterioration before targeted repair. Each method provides a different view of concrete condition and hidden damage.

UT
Ultrasonic

Detailed in-depth assessment for identifying internal anomalies, cracking, delamination, interfaces, and changes in concrete condition.

IE
Impact Echo

Reflected stress waves help locate internal cracks, voids, delamination, debonding, and structural thickness.

GPR
Subsurface Mapping

Maps subsurface features including reinforcement configuration, concrete cover, member thickness, voids, and deterioration.

IR
Rapid Screening

Rapid, non-contact screening of thermal anomalies associated with delamination, cracking, voids, and material changes.

Biological Restoration

Targeted healing inside the crack network.

Published laboratory research demonstrates that drilled vascular access can greatly improve transport of hydrogel-supported biological healing agents into existing subsurface crack networks.

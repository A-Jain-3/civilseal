01 / DETECT

Know where to look.

CivilSeal narrows large infrastructure networks down to the specific bridges and structural regions where detailed inspection and intervention matter most.

1A. Network Risk Prioritization

Rank structures using condition, materials, structural type, loading, age, repair history, environment, and hazard exposure.

1B. Critical Zone Prediction

Structural models and load paths identify regions where deterioration may be most consequential.

1C. Targeted Robotic NDE

Deploy complementary NDE methods only where detailed inspection is needed.

1D. Damage Prioritization

Evaluate severity, structural significance, progression risk, and repair suitability.

02 / RESTORE

Repair from within.

Targeted vascular access creates a pathway between the concrete surface and hidden crack networks, allowing biological healing agents to reach the damaged region directly.

Targeted Vascular Access

Engineered channels connect the surface to subsurface damage.

Hydrogel Delivery

Agar hydrogel carries bacteria and nutrients into the crack network.

MICCP

Ureolytic bacteria promote calcium carbonate precipitation within damaged concrete.

Internal Crack Sealing

Mineral precipitation develops throughout the targeted crack network.
